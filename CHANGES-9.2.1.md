# Saveit 9.2.1: drag layers in the Layers panel

- Press a layer and drag it up or down. A line shows where it will land, and the dragged layers fade while they move.
- Several selected layers (Shift or Ctrl+click) drag together and keep their order.
- A plain click selects; Shift or Ctrl+click adds or removes a layer.
- The list scrolls by itself when you drag near its top or bottom.
- Each drag is one Ctrl+Z.

Tested with real mouse drags: click, Ctrl+click, two layers dragged to the bottom, one layer dropped between two others, eye button still works. No errors.
