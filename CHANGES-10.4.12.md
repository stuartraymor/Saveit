# Saveit 10.4.12

## Effects are chosen in the bar under your selection, and they stack
- After you click **Capture** and drag an area, the bar under the selection (W × H, Size, Capture, Redo) shows the **effect** for this capture underneath, with all its settings beside it. The preview on the selection updates as you change them.
- **+ Add effect** (bottom right of that bar) adds another effect on top, for example Black & white plus a Border plus Rounded corners. Each effect has its own row; **✕** removes it. An effect that is already in the list is greyed out in the other rows.
- Your effects are remembered and used for the next capture. Full-screen captures show the same rows. Video recording and Grab text don't, because effects only apply to screenshots.

## More settings for every effect
- **Drop shadow:** Softness, Distance, Direction (below, below right, below left, all around), Darkness, Color.
- **Border:** Width, Color, Style (solid, dashed, dotted, double), Placement (around the picture or over its edge), Corners.
- **Rounded corners:** Radius, and which corners (all, top, bottom, left, right). While you adjust it, the cut-off corners are dimmed and a dotted line shows exactly where each corner is cut.
- **Torn / cut edge:** Style, Depth, and Size of the tears.
- **Black & white** and **Sepia:** Amount, Contrast, Brightness.
- **Vignette:** Strength, Size, Color.
- **Soften (blur):** Blur, Brightness, Saturation.
- **Brighten** and **More contrast:** Amount plus Contrast/Brightness and Saturation.
- **Color shift** (new): Hue and Saturation.
- **Invert colors:** an on/off switch.
- The new settings show the same in the editor, in exports and in the capture preview.

## Tighter capture bar in the editor and the widget
- The Effect drop-down and its ⚙ pop-up slider are gone from the editor's top bar and from the widget.
- Both bars use the same layout: **Image / Video / Audio, Cursor, Copy to clipboard** on the top row, **Selection** beside **Delay** underneath. In the widget, **Close** sits under Copy to clipboard.
- The Effects panel in the editor (for an image that is already captured) is unchanged.
