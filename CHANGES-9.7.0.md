# Saveit 9.7.0: layouts and backgrounds for recordings

A new **Layout** tab in the video editor, like Camtasia Rev. Every option shows a live preview made from your own video at the playhead, so you can see the result before you click.

- **Looks:** 12 one-click styles that set the layout and background together (Classic, Clean inset, Purple stage, Side by side, Presenter, Berry, Split, Ocean, Minimal white, Midnight, Peach, Steel screen).
- **Layout:** Screen only, Webcam in corner, Screen on background + webcam, Screen on background, Side by side (webcam right or left), Half and half, Presenter (webcam large) and Webcam only.
- **Background:** blank **Black**, blank **White**, 18 gradients, and a **Custom colour**. The background shows wherever the screen and webcam don't fill the frame.
- **Style:** webcam corner, shape (circle, rounded, square, wide), webcam size, rounded screen corners, shadow and webcam border.
- **Webcam as its own track:** recordings made with the webcam now keep it separate from the screen, so a layout can move and resize it. A new recording starts in the Classic layout, matching the webcam settings you recorded with. Older recordings (where the webcam is part of the picture) can use **Choose webcam video…** to add a separate webcam file.
- **Save** and copy-to-clipboard of a video with a webcam track or a layout now produce the finished video with the layout applied. Saving takes as long as the video.
- **Reset layout** goes back to the plain recording.

Also in this release (from 9.6.0): **Visual effects** for video. See [CHANGES-9.6.0.md](CHANGES-9.6.0.md).
