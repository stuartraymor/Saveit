# Saveit 10.4.10

## The page editor becomes a compact word processor
The Page button (and Grab Text) open the same editor. It keeps Saveit's dark look, now with Word-style tabs: **Home**, **Insert**, **Layout** and **Tools**.

### Spelling
- Right-click a word with a red underline to see suggestions in Saveit's own menu. Click one to fix the word, or choose **Add to dictionary**.
- **Tools > Spelling** turns the underlines on or off.

### Pictures
- Add pictures with **Insert > Picture**, by dropping them on the page, or by pasting. Large pictures are fitted to the page.
- Click a picture to select it. Drag a corner handle to resize it (it keeps its shape; hold Shift or use a side handle to stretch it). Drag the picture to move it.
- The picture bar and the right-click menu offer **Crop** (drag the box, then Apply crop or press Enter), **Rotate**, **In line**, **Wrap left**, **Wrap right**, **Centre**, **Reset size** and **Delete**.
- **Ctrl+Z** undoes every picture change.

### Writing and layout
- **Home** has paragraph styles, more fonts, sizes, bigger/smaller text (Ctrl+] and Ctrl+[), superscript and subscript, and colour palettes for text and highlight. It also has left, centre, right and justify alignment, bullet and numbering styles, and line and paragraph spacing.
- In a list, **Tab** and **Shift+Tab** move an item in and out a level. Elsewhere, Tab types a tab.
- **Insert** has tables (pick the size on a grid), links (Ctrl+K; Ctrl+click opens one), lines, page breaks (Ctrl+Enter), the date, symbols and tick boxes.
- In a table, Tab moves to the next cell and adds a row at the end. Right-click a table to insert or delete rows and columns, or delete the table.
- **Layout** sets the page size (Letter, A4, Legal), the orientation and the margins. The page on screen, printing and saved files all use these settings. It also has zoom and **Fit width**.
- The status bar shows words, characters and pages. **Tools > Word count** gives the details.

### Saving and opening
- **Save as** makes a **Word document (.docx)**, a **PDF**, a web page or a text file. The Word document keeps headings, fonts, colours, lists, tables, pictures (including wrapped ones), links and page settings.
- **Open** (Ctrl+O) opens Word documents (.docx), text files and web pages. You can also drop them on the window. **New** (Ctrl+N) starts a blank page.
- Printing now prints only the page, not the editor's buttons.
- Pages are kept in **Recent pages** even after Saveit closes or the computer restarts, so nothing you type is lost. **Remove** takes a page off the list (it asks first if the page is not saved in the library).

### Safety
- Pasted or opened content is cleaned of scripts, and the editor window blocks scripts that are not part of Saveit.
