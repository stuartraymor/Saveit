# Saveit 10.5.1

## No more flash when you click Capture
- The selection screen used to open with Windows' window-opening animation (a quick zoom and fade) and was already darkened, so the screen flashed between your desktop and the dimmed view.
- It now opens without the animation, showing the frozen screen exactly as it looked, and the dimming and the bar fade in over a fraction of a second.

## Every capture starts with No effect
- The effect rows under the selection always start at **No effect**. Effects you pick apply to that capture only and aren't carried over to the next one (or to window captures).
