Everything you need to explain your screen

Made for people who explain things to other people: work instructions, how-to guides, bug reports, training material, or a quick "click here" picture in an email.

Capture anything

A region, a window, the full screen, a whole scrolling page, or one shot per click. Grab Text reads words off the screen so you can paste them.

Mark it up

Arrows, callouts, text, shapes, numbered steps, stamps, highlight, blur, spotlight, magnify, crop and Magic Cut. Most things can be rotated.

Step-by-step guides

Click through a task and Saveit builds an illustrated guide, naming each step for you. Export to Word, PDF, PowerPoint or pictures.

Record and edit video

Record your screen with mic, system sound and webcam. Trim on a timeline, add captions, arrows, zooms and a voice-over. Save as MP4, WebM or GIF.

Record audio

Up to two microphones plus computer sound, each on its own track. Clean it up with noise reduction, EQ and voice presets. Save as WAV, MP3 or M4A.

Copy and paste anywhere

Copy the finished picture and paste it straight into Outlook, Gmail, Teams or any other app. It sits in the tray, so hotkeys always work.

Edit a recording

Trim and cut a screen recording on the timeline, then add a caption and a zoom to point out what matters.

Hide private information

Blur an email address and a secret key, then spotlight the button you want people to click.

Grab Text

Drag around any text in a picture and Saveit reads it for you, ready to edit and copy.

One small bar, always ready

Pick Image, Video or Audio, choose what to grab, and press Capture.

The editor: every tool has its own settings panel on the right.

Up and running in a minute

No admin rights needed. It installs just for you.

  1. Download and installClick Download for Windows, open the file, and click Install.
  2. CapturePress Print Screen, drag over what you want, and the editor opens with your picture.
  3. Mark it up and shareAdd an arrow or a callout, then click Copy and paste it into an email or chat, or click Save.
Print ScreenCapture a region
Shift + Print ScreenCapture a window
Ctrl + Print ScreenCapture the full screen
Ctrl + Shift + RStart or stop a screen recording
Ctrl + Alt + SStart a step-by-step guide
"Windows protected your PC"? Saveit isn't code-signed yet, so Windows may show this the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway. Saveit is open source, so anyone can read every line of the code.

Private by design

No account, no analytics, no tracking. Saveit never uploads your captures or recordings. It only goes online to download its runtime during setup, and when you click Check for Updates.
