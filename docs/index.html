Everything you need to explain your screen

Made for people who explain things to other people: work instructions, how-to guides, bug reports, training material, or a quick "click here" picture in an email.

Capture anything

A region, a window, the full screen, a whole scrolling page, or one shot per click. Grab Text reads words off the screen so you can paste them.

Mark it up

Arrows, callouts, text, shapes, numbered steps, stamps, highlight, blur, spotlight, magnify, crop and Magic Cut. Most things can be rotated.

Step-by-step guides

Click through a task and Saveit builds an illustrated guide, naming each step for you. Export to Word, PDF, PowerPoint or pictures.

Record and edit video

Record your screen with mic, system sound and webcam. Trim on a timeline, add captions, arrows, zooms and a voice-over. Save as MP4, WebM or GIF.

Record audio

Up to two microphones plus computer sound, each on its own track. Clean it up with noise reduction, EQ and voice presets. Save as WAV, MP3 or M4A.

Copy and paste anywhere

Copy the finished picture and paste it straight into Outlook, Gmail, Teams or any other app. It sits in the tray, so hotkeys always work.

Questions people ask

Quick answers about Saveit and what it can do.

Is there a free alternative to Snagit?

Yes. Saveit is a free, open-source Snagit alternative for Windows. It takes screenshots of a region, window, full screen or scrolling page, lets you add arrows, callouts, numbered steps and blur, records your screen with audio, and builds step-by-step guides from your clicks. There's no subscription, no account and no watermark.

Is Saveit really free?

Yes, completely. It's open source under the MIT license, so anyone can use it, share it and read the code. There are no ads, no trial period and no paid upgrade.

How do I blur private information in a screenshot?

Press Print Screen and drag over what you want to capture. In the editor, pick the Blur tool and drag over the email address, password or anything else you want hidden. Use Spotlight to dim everything except the part that matters.

How do I make a step-by-step guide with screenshots?

Press Ctrl + Alt + S and do the task as usual. Saveit captures a picture at every click and names each step for you ("Click Start"). When you stop, tidy up the steps and export the guide to Word, PDF, PowerPoint or images.

Can I record my screen with audio for free?

Yes. Saveit records your screen with your microphone, computer sound and webcam. You can then trim it on a timeline, add captions, arrows and zooms, and save it as MP4, WebM or GIF. It can also record audio on its own and save it as WAV, MP3 or M4A.

Why does Windows say "Windows protected your PC"?

Saveit isn't code-signed yet, so Windows doesn't recognise the publisher. Click More info, then Run anyway. All of the source code is public on GitHub, and in-app updates are checked with a digital signature before they install.

Does Saveit work on Mac or Linux?

Not at the moment. Saveit is made for Windows 10 and Windows 11.
