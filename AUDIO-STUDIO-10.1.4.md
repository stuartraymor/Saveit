# Try Audio studio

Open an audio item, then click **Master effects** to polish the complete mix. To work on one voice clip or track, select that clip or its track name first; the panel heading tells you what you are changing.

1. Choose **Clean narration** under Voice preset and click **Apply**. This adds a rumble filter, voice EQ, and gentle compression. Try Warm voice or Speech clarity as alternatives. Switching presets replaces the earlier preset's effects and keeps effects you added yourself.
2. Start playback. Click an effect in the chain to adjust it. **Open controls** gives you larger sliders and a response diagram. Changes are heard during playback.
3. Use **Bypass** for one effect or **Bypass rack** for the selected target to compare the sound. These switches keep your settings. Undo and Redo remain available after closing the expanded controls window.
4. Add **Peak limiter** if you need to contain loud peaks. A ceiling of −1 dBFS is a useful starting point. Watch both output meters and the held peak; use Clear peak to reset the warning after adjusting the sound.
5. **Normalize peaks** measures the mix and adds editable gain for the selected peak target. This adjusts sample peaks, not perceived loudness. Undo restores the previous level.
6. Save your audio as WAV, MP3, or M4A. Your editable effects remain in the Library item.

**Voice EQ:** Warmth changes the low-frequency body, Clarity changes the middle frequencies, and Brightness changes the top end. Make small adjustments and compare them with bypass.

**Compressor:** Threshold chooses where loud parts start being reduced. Ratio controls the reduction; Attack and Release control how quickly it responds. Output gain raises the processed level. Start with a preset and change one setting at a time.

**Editing:** Type selection From/To times in seconds, then use Play selection. Stereo clips show L/R separately. Waveform 2× or 4× enlarges the display without changing the audio. Track faders and clip gain use dB; double-click a track fader to return to 0 dB.

Original recordings are kept. Dedicated background-noise removal, LUFS loudness matching, and recording directly into the current project are for later updates.
