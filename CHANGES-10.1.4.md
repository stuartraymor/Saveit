# Saveit 10.1.4

- The Audio editor now has an Audio studio panel with colored effect cards, response diagrams, an editable effect chain, and a larger Open controls window.
- New voice tools: Rumble filter, Voice EQ, Compressor, Peak limiter, and exact gain in dB. Their settings are used for playback and saved audio, and can be changed or bypassed later.
- Clean narration, Warm voice, and Speech clarity presets add a starting chain for a voiceover. Applying another voice preset replaces that preset's effects while keeping effects you added yourself. Bypass rack lets you compare the current target with its effects switched off.
- Larger left/right output meters show separate channel levels, a held sample peak, and a clipping warning. Each track has a small level meter. Track and clip gain controls use dB, and double-clicking a track fader resets it to 0 dB.
- Normalize peaks measures the mix and adds an editable gain effect to reach −1, −3, or −6 dBFS. Undo restores the previous level. Editing is held while the mix is measured so the result stays consistent.
- Stereo clips show separate L/R waveforms. The waveform display can be enlarged without changing the sound.
- Type exact selection start/end times and use Play selection to check an edit. Existing move, trim, split, copy/paste, undo, and export actions remain available.

This update focuses on voiceovers and tutorials. Peak normalization measures sample peaks; LUFS loudness matching and true-peak measurement are future work. Rumble filtering reduces low frequencies; dedicated background-noise removal and de-essing remain future work, along with editable project-file export and recording into the current project. No cloud service or additional account is used.
