# Saveit 9.6.0: visual effects for video

A new **Visual effects** tab in the video editor works like an adjustment layer in Premiere Pro or CapCut. Each effect is a purple bar on its own **VISUAL EFFECTS** row in the timeline, and it changes the picture for as long as the bar lasts.

- **12 effects:** Brightness, Contrast, Exposure, Saturation, Black & white, Sepia, Invert, Hue shift, Blur, Warm, Cool, Vignette. Each button shows a small preview.
- **Any length:** click an effect to add it at the playhead (5 seconds). Drag its ends on the timeline, type from/to times, use Start here / End here, or click **Whole video**.
- **Stack them:** effects can overlap, so you can combine Brightness + Contrast, or Black & white for one part and Warm for another.
- **Fine control:** an Amount slider for each effect, plus Fade in / Fade out (like Camtasia's effect fades), Turn off, Duplicate and Remove.
- The list under the buttons shows every effect in the video. Click one to select it and jump to it.
- Effects change the video picture only. Captions and on-screen arrows and text stay unchanged.
- Effects move with your edits (delete, split, transitions, speed), work with Undo / Redo, are saved with the video, and are included when you save the edited video or a GIF.
