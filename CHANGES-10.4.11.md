# Saveit 10.4.11

## Effect moves to the bar under your selection
- After you click **Capture** and drag an area, the bar under the selection (W × H, Size, Capture, Redo) now has a second row: the **effect** for this capture, with its settings right beside it. For example, Border shows Width and Color, Rounded corners shows Radius, Torn / cut edge shows Style and Depth, Black & white shows Amount. The preview on the selection updates as you change them.
- The Effect drop-down and its ⚙ pop-up slider are gone from the capture widget and from the editor's top bar.
- The effect you pick there is remembered and used for the next capture, as before. Full-screen captures show the same row. Video recording and Grab text don't show it, because effects only apply to screenshots.
- The Effects panel in the editor (for an image that is already captured) is unchanged.
